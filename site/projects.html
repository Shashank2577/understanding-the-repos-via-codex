02 / THE NEXT MOVE

Start small.
Make the result inspectable.

  1. Ship an evidence review. Use Receipts and Sovix CLI with one fixed corpus, one disputed metric and reproducible arithmetic.
  2. Prove Cairn’s task benefit. Measure total cost and accepted-change time, including follow-up reads and enrichment.
  3. Close the meeting loop. Recording → cited decision → corrected knowledge → reviewed draft. Replays must not duplicate work.
See estimates, integration contracts and stop conditions ↗

HIGHEST STAKES

Platform, Foundry, OBSERVE

Potentially broad recurring value, with larger operational responsibilities: tenant isolation, agent authority and production diagnosis.

Before more scope: require an independent pilot, a clear failure test and a measured outcome.

Cairn and Meeting Intelligence offer narrower daily or weekly workflows to validate first.
